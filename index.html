What stays private?

Images and passwords are processed on your device. This app has no upload server, analytics, accounts or browser storage. GitHub serves the page and may log your visit; it does not receive your selected image or password from this app.

Picvert uses your browser’s AES-256-GCM encryption with a password-derived key. Use a strong, unique password: someone with the encrypted file can attempt password guesses offline. The file size is visible. The restored file includes its original metadata, such as any location information.

This is an unaudited tool. Its protection depends on your password, your device and the code delivered by this page. Images from the old six-character scrambling prototype are not compatible.
